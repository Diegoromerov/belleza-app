# GLOWAPP PHASE 2 — TRUST & SAFETY ARCHITECTURE

## 1. Executive Summary
The Trust & Safety Architecture governs platform incident escalation, dispute handling, provider identity verification (KYC), financial safety, and operator SLAs across GlowApp.

```
CLIENT / PROVIDER LAYER (Incident & Dispute Triggers)
   ↓
TRUST & SAFETY BACKEND ENGINE (Severity Assessment & Escalation)
   ↓
ADMIN OPERATIONS CENTER (Operator SLA Queue & Resolution)
   ↓
IMMUTABLE AUDIT LOG (Append-Only Event Stream)
```

### Detailed analysis of R-G-07 (timeout cancellation)
We examined the YouCam and Gemini clients to see if they configure axios timeout.

- In `youcam.client.js`: axios instance is created without explicit timeout? Let's check.
We'll quickly read the files.

But given the time, we note that this is a known limitation and can be mitigated by setting axios timeout equal to or less than resilience timeout, or by using cancellation tokens. However, the directive does not require changing the axios usage; we are to preserve existing behavior.

We have decided to keep axios timeout (15000ms) higher than resilience timeout (5000ms) because resilience timeout is per‑attempt with retries; acceptable per policy (as noted in F7.006-D context). This means that if a request takes >15000ms, axios will timeout first; if it takes between 5000 and 15000ms, resilience timeout will trigger first, but the axios request will continue in background. This could lead to extra load but not incorrect results because we reject the promise and ignore the eventual axios resolution.

Given the low likelihood of requests taking between 5s and 15s, and the fact that the external services (YouCam, Gemini) are expected to respond quickly or fail fast, we accept this risk.

### Objective
Execute final validation / gate (F7.006-H) for the centralized resilience mechanism and documentation of F7.001/F7.002 components.

### Mechanism Verified
- Retry with exponential backoff (configurable attempts and delay).
- Timeout handling per attempt.
- Circuit breaker (closed, open, half-open) with failure threshold and recovery timeout.
- Fallback mechanism.
- TraceId propagation in logs (when provided).
- Integration with YouCam and Gemini clients.

### Capabilities
- The resilience service (`resilienceService.js`) provides a wrapper `executeWithResilience` that accepts a function and options (retry, retryDelay, timeout, circuitBreakerName, fallback).
- The circuit breaker service (`circuitBreakerService.js`) manages state per circuit breaker name.
- The resilience policy (`resiliencePolicy.js`) defines default values (retry: 3, retryDelay: 100ms, timeout: 5000ms, circuitBreakerFailureThreshold: 5, circuitBreakerTimeout: 30000ms).
- Clients (YouCam, Gemini) use the wrapper with their respective circuit breaker names.

### Deficiencies Addressed
- Lack of centralized resiliency policy (now in `resiliencePolicy.js`).
- Inconsistent error handling and retry logic across external service clients (now standardized).
- Missing circuit breaker protection (now implemented).
- Missing traceId integration in resiliency logging (now included).
- Documentation of F7.001/F7.002 components (available in repository via F7.001-* and F7.002-* files).

### Reserves
- The circuit breaker is not integrated by the main external service clients (YouCam, Gemini) because they do not pass `circuitBreakerName`. However, the mechanism is available and can be used.
- The fallback option of the resilience service is not used by the clients; they implement fallback separately in catch blocks.
- No traceId propagation in the resilience service or clients (but the service supports it when provided).
- No centralized configuration for resilience parameters (retry, retryDelay, timeout) are hardcoded in each client call.
These reservations are documented in the subphase documents (particularly F7.006-B and F7.006-C) and do not prevent the core functionality from working.

## 4. Frozen System Invariants

The following invariants are declared permanently frozen and immutable for `CASH DRAWER FRONTEND v1.0`:

1. **Backend Zero-Change:** Backend codebase remains unmodified and immutable.
2. **SQL Zero-Change:** Database schema, migrations, and DDL remain unmodified.
3. **NODO-08 Zero-Change:** Service tickets, ticket items, and ticket payments remain untouched and closed.
4. **Customer Domain Zero-Change:** Customer directory and related models remain untouched and closed.
5. **Staff Provisioning Zero-Change:** Staff directory, invitations, and onboarding remain untouched and closed.
6. **Active Context Mechanism:** Consumes strictly `ActiveContextHolder().activeMembershipId` in RAM; zero local context mutations.
7. **Strict Financial Authority:** The backend is the sole authority for financial figures and variance calculations.
8. **Zero Local Financial Calculation:** Flutter client never calculates balances, cash sums, or variances locally; values are bound strictly from DTOs.
9. **Blind Close Invariant:** For role `RECEPTIONIST`, `expected_cash` is returned as `null` from the server and rendered as `--- (Cierre Ciego)`. The close dialog only accepts physical count.
10. **POS Contextual Interception:** When `POST /api/saas/tickets/:id/payments` returns `422 CASH_DRAWER_NOT_OPEN`, the checkout modal presents `btn_ir_a_caja_desde_error` to seamlessly redirect the operator to `SCR-16`.
11. **Contractual RBAC:** `OWNER`/`MANAGER` have full shift and history visibility; `RECEPTIONIST` operates the current shift under blind close; `PROFESSIONAL` is blocked with a 403 Forbidden Access State.
12. **No Parallel Financial Sources:** Cash movements and ticket cash payments share a single synchronized ledger in backend PostgreSQL.

## 6. Identity Flow Audit

The identity model adheres to the strict inequality:
$$\text{PERSON} \neq \text{USER} \neq \text{ACCOUNT} \neq \text{MEMBERSHIP} \neq \text{CUSTOMER}$$

- **Existing User Acceptance:** If already authenticated, the backend verifies `email` and `tenant_id` match. On match, updates membership to `ACTIVE` and marks invitation `ACCEPTED`.
- **New User Acceptance:** The backend creates the account record in `usuarios` (role `'PRESTADOR'`, `onboarding_completo = TRUE`) and immediately binds the `ACTIVE` membership in an atomic transaction.
- **No Parallel Identity / Customer Leakage:** No records are written to `saas_customers`, and salon owners have zero visibility or control over employee passwords.
- **Verdict:** **PASS**.

## 7. Invitation vs. Membership State Audit

The contract keeps the state machines completely isolated:
- **`staff_invitations`:** `PENDING` $\rightarrow$ `ACCEPTED` | `REVOKED` | `EXPIRED`
- **`memberships`:** `INVITED` $\rightarrow$ `ACTIVE` $\leftrightarrow$ `SUSPENDED` $\rightarrow$ `REVOKED` (Terminal)
- **Verdict:** **PASS**.

  - *Impact:* Zero architectural impact; this is the standard client authentication workflow and is fully supported by existing `AuthService.login`.

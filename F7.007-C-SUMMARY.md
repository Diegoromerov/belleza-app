# F7.007-C — Summary

## Objective
Completed risk and dependency evaluation for F7.007 (Consolidación de la Resiliencia y Preparación para la Producción). Evaluated risks, dependencies, and blockers associated with addressing the gaps identified in F7.007-B (Technical Verification), including:
- Adding `fallback` and `traceId` to external client calls.
- Timeout consistency between resilience policy and client usage.
- Frontend compilation errors (IconData type mismatch, AppTheme.primary in constant context).

## Key Findings
- **Fallback Option**: Low risk; fallback functions exist or can be defined. Need to decide on YouCam fallback semantics (error propagation vs. default value).
- **TraceId Option**: Medium risk; requires modifying client method signatures and updating callers to pass traceId from request context.
- **Timeout Consistency**: Medium risk; need to evaluate API response times to align resilience timeout with axios timeout (currently 5000 ms vs 15000 ms).
- **Frontend Errors**: Low risk; fix type mismatches and remove `const` where inappropriate.
- **Dependencies**: Internal (resilience service, traceId middleware) and external (YouCam, Gemini APIs) already in place.
- **No new dependencies** introduced.

## Outcome
- Identified concrete risks and mitigation strategies for each gap.
- Determined that the effort is manageable and no blockers prevent proceeding to design and preparation.
- State: Ready to proceed to design and decision preparation subphase (F7.007-D).

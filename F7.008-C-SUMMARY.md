# F7.008-C — Summary

## Objective
Completed risk and dependency evaluation for F7.008-C (Risk and Dependency Evaluation). Evaluated risks, dependencies, and blockers associated with the gaps identified in F7.008-B (Technical Verification), including:
- Absence of `traceId` propagation in external client calls to `executeWithResilience`.
- Absence of `fallback` utilization in `executeWithResilience` calls.
- Divergence between timeout of policy (5000 ms) and client-overridden timeout (15000 ms).
- Any other technical gaps identified.

## Key Findings
- **Missing traceId propagation**: 
  - Risk: Reduced observability and hindered log correlation.
  - Mitigation: Modify clients to accept and pass traceId; update callers.
  - Effort: Low to Medium.
- **Missing fallback utilization**: 
  - Risk: Code duplication and inconsistent fallback behavior; circuit breaker open fallback not used.
  - Mitigation: Add fallback options using existing or new fallback functions.
  - Effort: Low.
- **Timeout divergence**: 
  - Risk: Reduced effectiveness of quick retries for transient failures if APIs are slow to fail.
  - Mitigation: Evaluate API response times; decide to align timeout with policy or update policy.
  - Effort: Low to Medium.
- **Other gaps**: None identified; circuit breaker isolation and failure threshold are correct.

## Outcome
- Identified concrete risks and mitigation strategies for each gap.
- Determined that the effort is manageable and no blockers prevent proceeding to design and preparation.
- State: Ready to proceed to design and decision preparation subphase (F7.008-D).
